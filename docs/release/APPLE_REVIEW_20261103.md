2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Rimba is a productivity/focus app for people studying, working or building calmer focus routines. Timed focus activity grows a virtual forest sanctuary. History, calendar, quests and reflective content help users follow progress. Trees, islands, Soul and XP are virtual; purchases do not fund real-world tree planting. Rimba does not provide medical treatment.

3. ACCESS AND MAIN FEATURES
Use the demo email/password in the dedicated App Review fields. This standard non-admin account contains fictional data. Choose Sign In for existing email accounts; Join Sanctuary creates an account. Email verification accepts 8-digit codes. Apple and Google sign-in are also supported. Guest Mode supports local focus activity without registration.
From the sanctuary, choose a duration and Start Focus; pause/resume or cancel from the timer. The top menu opens Profile, Focus Stats, Journal & Quests, Share Island, audio/settings and Rimba Pro.
Campfire (Focus Together) requires sign-in. Create a room and share its invitation with another authenticated user, or paste an invitation to join. In the room's participant list, another participant has Report and Block controls. Report submits the chosen reason for operator review. Block and leave removes you from that room and prevents sharing rooms with that user. Report before blocking because blocking ends your membership. No public feed or free-text chat is provided. Operator moderation is not available to the demo account.
Rimba Pro offers monthly/annual auto-renewing subscriptions and a one-time Pioneer Lifetime purchase through Apple IAP. Restore Purchases is on the Pro screen. After verified Pro activation, the audio mixer permits two ambient sounds. Purchases and Restore require network access; cloud saves and Campfire also require internet. Core timer/local sanctuary activity works offline.
Delete Account & Data is in My Profile and requires confirmation. Deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription. Please use a disposable account for deletion so the supplied demo account remains available.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Supabase: email/account authentication, cloud saves, Campfire and moderation, server entitlement reconciliation. Apple Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In: optional identity providers. Apple StoreKit/App Store IAP: payments and restores. RevenueCat: native purchase integration, entitlement status and backend purchase-event reconciliation. No generative AI or advertising service delivers the core focus/forest functionality.

5. REGIONS
The feature set is consistent across offered territories. English and Indonesian are supported. Storefront prices/currencies and purchase availability follow Apple storefront settings. There is no geographic variation in focus/Campfire content; distribution is limited to the territories configured in App Store Connect.

6. REGULATED SERVICES / CONTENT RIGHTS
This is a general productivity app, not a regulated medical, financial or gambling service. The developer has confirmed that bundled artwork, models, audio and other materials are owned or licensed for use in Rimba. No protected third-party service or real-world planting benefit is sold.
